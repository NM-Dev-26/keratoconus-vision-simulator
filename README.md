## Overview

**CorneaSim** is a real-time clinical corneal topography and wavefront optics simulator designed to help keratoconus patients, optometrists, ophthalmologists, and families visualize how irregular corneal ectasia distorts human vision.

### Key Capabilities

- **Interactive 2D & 3D Corneal Topography**:
  - Sculpt Pentacam-style **Axial / Sagittal Curvature** and **Anterior Elevation** maps in real time, or **upload your own clinical corneal topography scan** (with automatic circle detection and printed-label filtering).
  - Inspect the cornea in **Real Eye 3D** (with anatomical iris and dilating pupil), **Topo 3D** mesh, or **2D Cross-Section Slice** with live ray tracing.
- **Clinical Keratoconus Morphology Presets**:
  - *Normal 20/20*, *Oval Cone (Inferotemporal)*, *Skewed Radial Axes (SRAX)*, *Central Nipple Cone*, *Snowman / Duck*, *Crab-Claw (Pellucid Marginal Degeneration — PMD)*, and *Globus Generalized Ectasia*.
- **Contact Lens & Spectacle Optics on the Cornea**:
  - Compare **No Lens (Naked Eye)**, **Spectacles (Glasses)**, **Soft Toric Lenses**, **Corneal RGP Lenses**, **Standard Scleral Lenses** (including posterior corneal residual coma inversion), and **Wavefront-Guided (WFG) 20/20 Scleral Lenses**.
- **6 High-Contrast Clinical & Daily-Life Visual Scenes**:
  - *Night Highway Driving (Headlights & Road Signs)*
  - *Snellen Visual Acuity Chart (20/200 – 20/20)*
  - *Movie / TV Subtitles (White on Dark)*
  - *Smartphone & Book Reading (Dark Mode vs. Light Mode)*
  - *Coding IDE & Terminal Monitor*
  - *Night Street Signals & Digital Clock*
